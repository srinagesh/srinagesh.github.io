About

I'm a Principal Applied Scientist and Technical Advisor in Microsoft's Office of the CTO, where I advise on post-training research.

Before this, I led a team at Microsoft Turing post-training frontier reasoning models for Copilot, and was science lead for Expert Answers in Microsoft 365 Copilot Tuning, now Frontier Tuning, which post-trains a specialized models for enterprises.

I did my Ph.D. at the University of Michigan, where I was advised by Clayton Scott and James Cutler. I worked on machine learning for spacecraft: contextual bandits for adaptive sensor selection, kernel methods for tracking swarms of satellites, and the sensor and communication systems for six nanosatellite missions as part of MXL.
